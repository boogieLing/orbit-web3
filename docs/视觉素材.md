## 主视觉制作描述

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a wide hero artwork for UsedIt, a sophisticated consumer Web3 application discovery product. Create a striking premium 3D studio render: one monumental folded continuous chrome ribbon forming an open sculptural portal, with a narrow translucent chartreuse glass inner edge. Physically convincing brushed and mirror polished silver, crisp reflections, intricate subtle machining, weight and depth. Floating slightly above a dark graphite studio floor with a soft grounded shadow. Three-quarter perspective. Architectural rather than jewelry. Composition: wide landscape 3:2, object placed in the right two thirds with generous completely dark negative space at left for a website headline; sculpture fully contained, close framing but not clipped. Background almost black #101112, subtle neutral studio falloff. Highly art-directed industrial design campaign photography, dramatic silver highlights, restrained acid green accent, no purple or blue glow, no coins, crypto logos, charts, network nodes, sparkles, text, letters, watermarks, UI, borders. The result should look like an expensive contemporary design studio render, not a sci-fi wallpaper.

## 个人空间制作描述

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: supporting art for a personal collection workspace inside UsedIt, a premium Web3 discovery product. A sculptural stack of five large thin rounded rectangular slabs, alternately polished brushed aluminum and translucent smoky glass, arranged like carefully collected objects. One slender acid-chartreuse edge in the middle slab. Dramatic three-quarter overhead perspective. Photo-real industrial design studio render with precise chamfered edges, fine machining, tactile material, controlled silver reflections. The slabs gently fan apart with coherent perspective and real depth. Centered object on a seamless very dark graphite #101112 backdrop with soft grounded shadow, wide landscape 3:2 composition, ample negative space surrounding it. Monochromatic silver/black with only the single chartreuse accent. Professional restrained Silicon Valley hardware campaign art. No text, letters, logos, cryptocurrency coins, UI, symbols, network lines, stars, watermarks, blue or purple lighting.
